    Compliance + controls

    Built for South African HR & finance.

    POPIA, FICA, BEE — we know the checklist.

    POPIA-compliant

    Employee bookings, medical notes and payment data are processed per POPIA. Data export and deletion on 30-day response.

    FICA for high-value spend

    KYB identity verification on Corporate accounts. Beneficial-ownership capture in the dashboard.

    Per-employee monthly caps

    Set a Rand limit per person per month. Employees see their remaining balance in-app.

    Department analytics

    Spend by department, cost centre or category. Exportable CSV for finance reporting.

    Beneficial-ownership support

    UBO registration captured up-front for regulated industries. Update any time from Settings.

    Paystack-backed

    All payments processed by Paystack (PCI DSS Level 1, 3D Secure). Cards, instant EFT and wallet top-ups.
